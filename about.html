About the photographer

Kamil Salameh is a Lebanese photographer specializing in landscape photography, astrophotography, architectural and street photography, and fine art. He is the founder of Kamil Salameh Photography, a fine art photography studio based in Lebanon that creates cinematic visual stories across Lebanon and around the world.

Lebanon, seen slowly

Much of Kamil Salameh's Lebanese work is made in Mount Lebanon, around Kfardebian. There he photographs the Roman temple ruins of Faqra in the last light of the day, and the dark mountain skies above them, including the Neowise comet. His landscapes favour patience over spectacle: waiting for the light, composing carefully, and developing each image with care in Adobe Lightroom.

A portfolio beyond borders

Beyond Lebanon, his portfolio follows architecture, streets and landscapes across France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany, Denmark, Sweden and Norway, as well as Turkey, Armenia and the wildlife of the Masai Mara in Kenya. From the riverside facades of Dinant to the colourful harbour of Copenhagen, the work is united by a cinematic, emotional approach: photography is not about capturing moments, it is about preserving emotions.

Craft and process

Kamil works with Canon cameras and develops every photograph in Adobe Lightroom. Alongside landscape and astrophotography, he creates product and still-life photography for brands, and architectural images for businesses.

Prints, gifts and commissions

Kamil Salameh Photography produces fine art photographic prints and canvas from A5 to A0, framed or unframed, as well as wooden desk frames, coaster sets, collector puzzles and bespoke memory books. The studio also accepts commercial and architectural projects and private photography sessions. Every request receives a personal quote: request a print or commission.

Professional background

Alongside photography, Kamil Salameh is Assistant Director of Billing at TotalCare, with experience in healthcare billing management, departmental strategy, leadership and mentorship. More on LinkedIn.
